No: a creature cannot hide from a creature that can see it clearly, regardless of its Stealth roll. The DM decides when circumstances permit hiding. Cover or obscurement may make hiding possible, but are not universal sufficient conditions, and special features can change the requirements. An invisible creature can try to hide without visual cover; invisibility alone does not conceal its noises or automatically make its location unknown. Do not label a house rule about distractions as official intent.
