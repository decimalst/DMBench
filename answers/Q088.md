Tiny Hut does not prohibit casting spells wholly inside it, provided all other casting requirements are met. It prohibits spells and other magical effects extending through or being cast through the dome, in either direction. It also ends when its caster leaves its area. Teleportation Circle takes 1 minute and creates a short-lived portal to a destination circle; Tiny Hut does not explicitly name this interaction. Whether that portal counts as a magical effect extending through the dome needs a reasoned DM interpretation, rather than a categorical claim that casting from inside bypasses the barrier. If the DM allows passage and the hut's caster leaves through it, the hut ends. Grade the explicit restrictions and recognition of the interpretive issue, not a forced yes/no teleport ruling.
