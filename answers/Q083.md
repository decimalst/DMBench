Simulacrum explicitly permits repair in an alchemical laboratory using 100 gp of rare herbs and minerals per hit point restored. The spell does not contain the old key's blanket prohibition on regaining hit points; it does prohibit regaining expended spell slots. Wish has a listed, non-duplication option that restores all hit points to up to twenty visible creatures, without excluding constructs, so a visible simulacrum is eligible under that text. This use incurs Wish's stress and its 33 percent risk of losing the ability to cast Wish again. Duplicating Cure Wounds does not help a construct, which that spell excludes. A wish for some further outcome beyond the listed effects is subject to DM adjudication. Restoring hit points does not restore expended spell slots.
