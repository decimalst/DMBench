Legendary Resistance can turn the failed saving throw into a success, spending one available use and preventing Turn Undead's effect. If the failure stands, the creature is turned for 1 minute or until it takes damage, unless Destroy Undead applies. A cleric with Destroy Undead instead instantly destroys an undead whose challenge rating is at or below that cleric level's threshold when it fails the save. Legendary Resistance is not a separate saving throw and is not automatic immunity.
