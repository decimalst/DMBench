A grapple replaces one attack within the Attack action; it requires a free hand, a target within reach, and a target no more than one size larger than the grappler. Resolve Strength (Athletics) versus the target's choice of Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics). Prone alone does not impose disadvantage on these ability checks. On success, the target's speed is 0, so it cannot stand while grappled. The grappler can move the target, but its speed is halved unless the target is at least two sizes smaller. An escape normally costs the target an action and a successful contested check.
