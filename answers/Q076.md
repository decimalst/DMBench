Subtle Spell removes verbal and somatic components, not material components. Hold Monster has a material component, visibly handled in this scenario, so Subtle Spell alone does not prevent Counterspell. The enemy must still meet its reaction, sight, and 60-foot-range requirements. A 3rd-level Counterspell needs a DC 15 spellcasting-ability check against this 5th-level spell; a slot of 5th level or higher counters it automatically. A subtle spell with no material component has no perceptible casting for Counterspell to target. Do not invent an automatic spell-identification check or confuse this reaction with the Ready action.
