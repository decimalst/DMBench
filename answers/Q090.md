Resolve each interrupting Counterspell before the spell it is attempting to interrupt. For example, A casts a spell, B counterspells A, and C counterspells B: resolve C first. If C successfully counters B, B does not counter A, so A can proceed. If C fails, resolve B against A. Each attempt must meet sight, range, slot, and reaction requirements; higher-level targets may require checks. A caster can counter a Counterspell on its own turn if it has a reaction, subject to the same-turn bonus-action spell restriction. Once a reaction is spent, another is unavailable until the start of that creature's next turn. Sharing an initiative count does not supply extra reactions.
