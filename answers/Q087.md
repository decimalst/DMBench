No opportunity attack is provoked from that Sentinel user by this movement: Mobile's protection follows a melee attack against that creature whether it hits or misses. Sentinel's Disengage exception does not cancel Mobile in the stated scenario. If Mobile's protection was not established against that user and no other exception applies, leaving reach can provoke normally; a Sentinel opportunity attack that hits reduces the mover's speed to 0 for the rest of that turn. Mobile does not impose disadvantage on opportunity attacks or provide immunity to speed reduction on a hit.
