At wizard level 6, the revised Bladesinger can take the Attack action, make one ordinary weapon attack, and cast one cantrip in place of the other attack. For example, attack with a rapier, then cast Shocking Grasp against a target in reach using a free hand for its somatic component. These use the same Attack action and need no bonus action. This substitution is a feature of the revised text; it is not granted by ordinary Extra Attack.
