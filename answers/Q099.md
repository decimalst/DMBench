Adamantine armor converts critical hits into normal hits; Heat Metal uses no attack roll and therefore no critical hit. A 2nd-level casting deals 2d8 fire damage to a creature in contact with the armor immediately. On each subsequent turn the caster can use a bonus action to deal that damage again while concentration lasts. A creature holding or wearing the object and taking damage must save with Constitution or drop it if it can; armor cannot simply be dropped. If it does not drop the object, it has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the start of the caster's next turn. Repeating the damage can reapply this effect; it is not an unconditional disadvantage lasting until the armor is removed.
