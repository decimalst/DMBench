Dropping prone on your turn costs neither an action nor any of your speed. The prone condition does not grant advantage on Dexterity saves or reduce Lightning Bolt's damage; the specified line still includes the creature. Its attack-roll effects are irrelevant to this saving-throw spell. Standing costs movement equal to half your speed, and is impossible if your speed is 0 or insufficient movement remains. Dropping prone is not a general off-turn reaction to a spell.
