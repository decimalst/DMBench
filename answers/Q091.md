A 3rd-level casting creates one skeleton or zombie, or instead reasserts control over up to four creatures animated with the spell before their current 24-hour control expires. Creation and renewal are alternative uses, not both at once. Each slot level above 3rd adds two creatures to the chosen creation or renewal capacity: a 4th-level casting creates three or renews six. Separate castings can maintain separate groups, so there is no global cap of three times spell level. Control lasts 24 hours and each group must be renewed in time. Slot availability, eligible remains, and the renewal schedule limit the practical total.
