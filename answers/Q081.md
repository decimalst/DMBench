While mounted and not incapacitated, the 2014 feat gives advantage on melee attack rolls against unmounted creatures smaller than the mount. It does not give attackers disadvantage against the rider or give the rider advantage on Dexterity saves. For example, a rider on a Large horse attacks an unmounted Medium enemy while the rider is poisoned: the feat's advantage and poisoned's disadvantage cancel. Its other benefits allow redirecting attacks from mount to rider and improve the mount's outcome on qualifying Dexterity saves; those are not the invented rider defenses in the old key.
