## Scenarios (Q076–Q100)

76. **Counterspell vs. Subtle Spell:** A sorcerer casts Hold Monster using Subtle Spell while visibly handling its material component. An enemy who can see the sorcerer is within 60 feet and has a reaction available. Is the spell automatically immune to Counterspell? Contrast a subtle spell with no material component.

77. **Haste ends mid-turn:** A hasted rogue uses the extra action to Attack; the wizard's concentration then ends. Explain the effect on the rogue's remaining movement, action, and bonus action this turn and on the next turn. Is there an additional speed-halving penalty?

78. **Concentration after damage:** A druid with Constitution 17, no Constitution-save proficiency, and no other modifiers takes 28 damage from one source while concentrating on a Polymorph they cast on an ally. The druid remains conscious. What is the save DC and modifier, and what happens on success or failure?

79. **Throwing Alchemist's Fire:** Describe the action, attack ability, maximum throwing distance specified by the item, and whether proficiency with alchemist's supplies alone adds the proficiency bonus. When does the item's listed fire damage first occur, and what check can end it? You need not adjudicate damage-modifier debates.

80. **Sneak Attack with Ready:** A rogue takes the Ready action to shoot on a later creature's turn. Can an ordinary rogue also take the Attack action on that same turn? Explain how a rogue/fighter with Action Surge could attack on its turn and ready a shot, applying Sneak Attack to both hits if all its requirements are met.

81. **Mounted Combatant:** Describe the feat's 2014 melee-attack advantage benefit while mounted and not incapacitated. Does the feat itself impose disadvantage on attacks against the rider? Give an example of the rider's advantage being canceled by disadvantage.

82. **Devil's Sight and Darkness:** A warlock with the Devil's Sight invocation and an enemy with ordinary darkvision are within 30 feet of each other inside magical Darkness. Neither has another special sense or cover. Describe what each can see and the effect on attacks between them.

83. **Repairing a Simulacrum:** Describe the repair method explicitly given by Simulacrum. Compare it with Wish's listed option to restore all hit points to creatures the caster can see. Does duplicating Cure Wounds necessarily achieve the same thing? Distinguish those listed effects from an open-ended wish adjudicated by the DM.

84. **Grappling a prone creature:** Describe how a normal grapple attempt uses the Attack action, how it is resolved, and the movement effects on both creatures if it succeeds. When is the grappler exempt from the normal dragging speed penalty?

85. **Goodberry in a Bag of Holding:** Does the bag preserve a berry's magical nourishment and healing beyond 24 hours? Distinguish expiration of its magic from any assumed physical decay.

86. **Wild Shape at 0 hit points:** A druid drops to 0 hit points in beast form and is healed later that round. Explain reversion, excess damage, and which form receives the healing. Assume the druid survives the damage.

87. **Sentinel vs. Mobile:** A creature with the 2014 Mobile feat makes a melee attack against a Sentinel user, misses, then moves out of that user's reach on the same turn without taking Disengage. Does it provoke an opportunity attack from that user? Contrast moving away without having attacked that user.

88. **Teleportation Circle inside Tiny Hut:** Can a spell be cast entirely inside the hut? State the hut's restriction on magic crossing its dome and what happens if the hut's caster leaves. For an outward Teleportation Circle portal, distinguish explicit spell text from interpretation of whether teleportation crosses the dome; do not assume all spells cast inside bypass the restriction.

89. ***Revivify* after *Disintegrate*:** Explain why it succeeds or fails.

90. **Counterspell chain reactions in the same initiative count.** How is order resolved?

91. **Animate Dead control:** At base level, how many undead can one casting create, or alternatively reassert control over? How do higher slots change those numbers? Can repeated castings maintain more than one casting's limit, and how long does control last?

92. **Interrupted long rest:** Two separate fights, each lasting a full hour, interrupt an attempted eight-hour long rest. Does it qualify? Apply the 2014 long-rest interruption threshold.

93. **Hiding in the open:** Can an ordinary visible creature hide from an observer who sees it clearly merely by rolling high on Stealth? Explain the DM's role and why invisibility is different from automatically being hidden.

94. **Bladesinger Extra Attack:** Use the 2020 revised Bladesinger feature, as incorporated in the 2021 Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide errata. Give a legal level-6 Attack action that substitutes a cantrip for one attack; do not use the original unamended 2015 feature.

95. **Silvered weapon vs. a devil:** A barbed devil resists bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren't silvered. What damage does a nonmagical silvered longsword deal, absent other modifiers? Does silvering make the weapon magical?

96. **Potion miscibility:** Under the 2014 DMG variant for mixing potions, what can the DM do when a character drinks a second potion while the first still has an effect? Is this an obligatory rule in every game, and is the result always harmful? Exact table ranges are not required.

97. **Swimming in heavy armor:** A proficient wearer of plate meets its Strength requirement and is not encumbered. Does plate itself impose disadvantage on Athletics checks to swim, or make swimming impossible? Explain normal swimming movement and when the DM might call for a check.

98. **Prone vs. Lightning Bolt:** On your turn, you drop prone before a later Lightning Bolt whose line still includes you. What action or movement does dropping prone cost, does prone itself help the Dexterity save, and what does standing up cost?

99. **Heat Metal on adamantine armor:** A creature wears metal adamantine armor affected by a 2nd-level Heat Metal. Explain damage on casting and later turns, the armor's critical-hit protection, and when the spell imposes disadvantage. Assume concentration continues.

100. **Turn Undead vs. Legendary Resistance:** An undead creature fails the Wisdom save against Turn Undead. How can an available Legendary Resistance change the outcome? When would Destroy Undead apply instead of turning?
